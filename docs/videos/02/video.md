# Original Description

Please create a 'video' that goes through the whole game in a very commercial way.

It should start with with a camer zooming in on an old CRT TV showing a classic pong game being played. The typical CRT effects of a tv from 1972 should be shown. (e.g. the scan lines)

The TV fades and the number "1972" comes into view in large letters, very Atari style. The numbers start spinning to 2026, first slow and then faster but the numbers should still be recognizable.
As we hit 2026, they pulsate and then fade away.

It then switches over to a user journey into the Pong2000 game, creating the profile and showing the player being on the bottom of the Leaderboard at around position 99. Zoom into the ranking of the user

We now start the matches, winning matches and with each match the player rises in the leaderboard. Show a 2 by 2 matrix where each field is an individual game session. Then it changes to a 4 by 4 matrix. Then a 8 by 8 matrix.

The user hits place 1. We zoom from position 99 all the way up the leaderboard to position 1 with the gold trophy! The trophy should pulsate . 
Then we overlay the sentence: WINNER WINNER CHICKEN DINNER

Then the scene calms down again and we have just a single interactive match shown slowly running. Paddles moving and the ball going from left to right and back. This match is slowly fading behind a milky screen. Then in front of the milky screen, not hidden by the milky screen we see the question: "Ready to play?"

The question fades away and is replaced by "Pong2000"

# Sequence Breakdown

- **1972 CRT TV Zoom-In** (0.0s–3.1s): Opens in a dimly lit room on a vintage walnut 1972 CRT television with rotary dials, speaker grille, rabbit-ear antenna, scanlines, glass glare, and a moving analog raster line while a classic Pong match plays inside; the camera zooms straight into the CRT screen before fading out.
- **1972 → 2026 Atari Evolution** (3.1s–9.2s): Large Atari-style `1972` block numerals appear and spin upward on a mechanical reel—slow at first, accelerating smoothly while staying readable—then land on `2026`, pulsate twice in emerald glow, and fade out.
- **Profile Creation & Position #99 Zoom** (9.2s–15.4s): Follows **Alex** creating a profile with the `Rally Fox` emblem, then lands on the bottom of a 99-player Leaderboard where the camera zooms directly onto `#99 Alex · You`.
- **Escalating Match Matrices** (15.4s–25.2s): Expands through live simultaneous Pong2000 sessions—starting at **2×2** (4 arenas), splitting to **4×4** (16 arenas), and scaling to **8×8** (64 arenas)—while the top rank ticker climbs from `#99` up to `#1`.
- **#99 → #1 Climb, Pulsating Gold Trophy & Celebration** (25.2s–31.6s): Scrolls rapidly all the way up the Leaderboard from `#99` to `#1`, zooms onto the `#1` row with a visibly pulsating Gold Trophy, and overlays `#1 PLACE · CHAMPION` and `WINNER WINNER CHICKEN DINNER` with celebratory confetti.
- **Slow Interactive Match → Milky Screen Outro** (31.6s–39.6s): Settles onto a single slow-motion interactive match with paddles rallying the ball back and forth, then gradually blurs and fades the match behind a translucent milky frosted screen while crisp `Ready to play?` text in front fades into `Pong2000`.
